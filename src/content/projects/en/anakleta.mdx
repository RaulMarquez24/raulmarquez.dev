## Why it exists

Running an active Clash of Clans clan means keeping track of dozens of players: who attacks in war, who signs up for the league, who hasn't shown up for days. I built it for my clan. It brings all of that into a private panel for the co-leaders and hands the repetitive work to a Discord bot.

## What it does

- **Panel:** members with their history and linked accounts, activity and streaks, wars attack by attack, warnings and sanctions, league sign-ups and seasons, rankings and capital raids. Plus a rules editor and an announcement composer for Discord.
- **Bot:** league sign-up by typing naturally ("I'm in") or with commands, a welcome by direct message, and nickname and roles set from the player's game account.

## Architecture

The panel (Next.js 16 with Server Actions, on Supabase) and the bot (Node with discord.js) are deployed separately and only talk through the database. There are no queues: GitHub Actions triggers the scheduled jobs against protected panel routes. There's a clan snapshot every six hours, war capture and reminders every hour, and daily syncs. All of them are idempotent, so re-running one never duplicates anything.

## Decisions

**Understanding free text without false positives.** The bot recognizes intent despite typos, but only applies edit distance to long, specific words. On short ones, a typo would end up triggering actions nobody asked for.

**Telling inactivity from a season reset.** Periodic clan snapshots show whether someone has stopped playing or the game has simply reset the season.

**Independent pieces.** Since the panel and the bot only share the database, either can be updated or restarted without affecting the other.

## Outcome

In production for my clan since summer 2026. It's tailored to it, but it can be adapted to any other. Like everything I build on my own, it comes from automating a real need, and I publish it so others can use it.
